EXPERIMENT ARRANGEMENT
Two comparison rounds
Round A executes every proposal and only observes the review. Round B enforces it.
Decision rules
Behavior proposes ON at balanced accuracy ≥ 80% with at most 1 omission, and OFF at ≤ 60% or 2 omissions. Round B opens only when behavior, the workload score and gaze agree. It defers a workload-only OFF when balanced accuracy is ≥ 80%, eye coverage ≥ 70% and task gaze ≥ 60%, plus timing checks. Clips continue across cuts while ON; OFF keeps the playback position.
