## Current personal classifier

From 2026-11-10 the service trains a within-person random forest, revision `rest-math-iaf-rf-v3-5s`. The Unity calibration revision is `vermont-180s-math-occasional-decoy-v10`. Use Calibration only to save the model, then select it for the same participant in a Bioadaptive engineering test. The Physiology page displays the arithmetic-class score, estimated individual alpha frequency (IAF), and mean held-out AUC. A larger score indicates that the recorded features resemble this person's arithmetic condition more closely. In the single-period test the score is observational. In Adaptive Comparison, choose **A/B with a saved model** to use the score for decisions in both periods. The engineering route uses the existing OR rule in A and adds the evidence checks in B. Engineering comparison scoring and its pre-Unity preview now use all five epochs after resampling and filtering. The calibration training quality procedure below still applies to fitting. Restart the service and run fresh calibration; historical ten-second RF models and thresholds are not reused in this revision. [Window contract, migration and validation](FiveSecondWorkloadReview.md).

The method adapts the eight workload features and individual random forest from Sciaraffa et al. (2022), pp. 4–8. The paper used closed-eye rest for IAF and easy/hard multitasking for workload labels. This implementation estimates a peak during eyes-open forest viewing and labels the two existing conditions by stage. It uses spectral band power for each electrode and rejects artifacts without the paper's ocular correction. It uses class weights and fixed forest settings. These choices define a separate engineering model. [Original publication](https://doi.org/10.3389/fnhum.2022.901387).

- Evaluate up to 176 five-second intervals per 180-second condition at one-second steps with verified live 250 Hz Mindtooth channels, units, timing and intact mounting. Filter each window separately with a fifth-order 2–30 Hz Butterworth filter applied forward and backward. Test each one-second epoch across all eight channels for raw integrity, flat channels and excursions above ±80 µV. Use windows with at least three accepted epochs. For training, keep the earliest available usable window in each 60-second fold and skip candidates overlapping an already selected interval or crossing a fold boundary. This keeps at most thirty-six disjoint intervals per condition. Every candidate can update the operator warning. A rolling check allows one second for sample arrival, plus the console polling interval.
- Compute one-sided Hann power spectra at 1 Hz resolution. Estimate the resting posterior alpha peak from the median epoch spectrum after averaging Pz, P3 and P4. Search 7–13 Hz for the strongest local maximum with at least 15% prominence relative to the maximum over 6–14 Hz. Reject unclear peaks. For each epoch, integrate theta power over [IAF−6, IAF−2) at AFz, AF3, AF4, AF7, AF8 and alpha power over [IAF−2, IAF+2) at Pz, P3, P4.
- Fit 100 trees, maximum depth four, balanced class weights and the session seed. Validate on three consecutive sixty-second portions from each condition. Hold out corresponding portions of both conditions, estimate IAF and train only on the remaining portions, and average epoch scores within each held-out five-second window. Require at least twelve usable windows per condition, both conditions in each fold, and mean fold AUC of at least 0.65 before fitting to all accepted calibration windows. The report also records accepted one-second epoch totals. These acceptance settings are provisional.
- Inspect `load_windows.jsonl` for candidate timestamps, clean-second counts, flagged channel counts where available, selection status and exclusions. The service report in `load_reference.json` records the revision, features, counts, fold AUCs, IAF, failure reason or model digest. Successful fits write `workload_rf.joblib`. The engineering registry checks identity, signal contract, policy, software version and hashes on reuse. Historical anchor models retain their original scoring when explicitly selected.

A single exposure per condition in fixed order limits interpretation. Held-out portions of the same session can reflect task order, time or movement. Worn unit conversion, alpha-peak reliability, transfer to an independent 2-back session, headset acceptance and questionnaire suitability remain open.

## Video threshold recommendations

As of 2026-11-07, recommendations remain model-specific, but researcher-selected cutoffs are editable during either comparison round. Apply changes in Study setup; future decisions record their actual pair and revision. The initial selection and change history remain available in the session record. See [live threshold operation](LiveThresholdsReview.md).

For each successfully trained workload RF, each temporal fold model scores its held-out, non-overlapping forest and arithmetic windows on the deployment scale (all five epochs in `rest-math-iaf-rf-v3-5s`; historical v2 used ten). Screened training and AUC calculation are retained. Each accepted window contributes exactly one score. Counts compare the individual scores at full precision to inclusive cutoffs.

The engineering recommendation searches a 0.01 grid on either side of the midpoint of the two class medians. For ON it maximizes forest coverage while allowing at most 15% arithmetic triggers; for OFF it maximizes arithmetic coverage while allowing at most 10% forest triggers. Ties favor fewer opposite-condition hits and then proximity to the midpoint. If a cap cannot be met, the rule first minimizes its violation. These operating preferences and the displayed counts are exploratory calibration estimates. They do not measure in-task accuracy. Each model report includes the scores and recommendations. Older verified reports can carry a model-hash-bound threshold profile alongside the report.
