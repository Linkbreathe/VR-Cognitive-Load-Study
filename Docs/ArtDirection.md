# Quiet laboratory / presentation v1.1

Current v1.6: a fixed 0.65 m seated desk and a minimal running HUD with no large backplates. Compact stage/mode/color count and comparison text sit above the sphere; a small countdown sits to its right. Practice correctness uses green/red countdown text. The sphere stays visible throughout the four-second response interval. Earlier layout notes below are historical.

Current v1.6 approved experiment extension: one temporary silent street-video window at a seeded center/corner front-wall position, with distractions every three or four trials, rear door sounds, and quiet English two-speaker conversation beyond the rear wall. These scheduled formal-block distractions explicitly supersede the historical no-extra-audio/animated-scenery baseline below. Neutral room lighting, task stimuli and response feedback remain unchanged.

Current v1.3.1 stimulus refinement: user-approved satin finish using URP Lit, metallic 0 and smoothness 0.42 under the existing fixed lighting. A 64-segment, 32-ring smooth sphere preserves the radius and visual angle while removing coarse surface faceting. Base palette values are retained; shaded pixel values now vary across the surface. This supersedes the historical unlit-sphere choice below.

Current v1.3 extension: the stimulus floats independently over a matte laboratory workbench. Compact world-space signs show rule, countdown and progress; the full board is reserved for non-running phases. Existing colors and fixed lighting are preserved. One quiet onset cue per color is explicitly approved; the earlier no-audio rule below describes the v1.1 baseline. No other sound or scenery animation is introduced.

Style: a restrained architectural VR laboratory with a warm neutral shell, a thin matte task panel and precise English typography.
Palette: wall #8A8985, floor #555A5B, trim #6A6D6C, panel #30393D, inset #394347, text #F0F1ED, muted #B9C4C4; accent #A9C7BF appears only in preparation and instructional content.
Mood: stable diffuse overhead illumination, subtle material separation and joinery; no decorative animation, audio, bloom, vignette, automatic exposure or camera movement.

The four stimulus colors and unlit sphere are retained from v1.0. The static panel and room are identical in both conditions. The panel remains fixed in world space, with the sphere at the calibrated 1.5 m distance and 6 degree visual diameter.

Read craft references: `art-direction.md`, `scene-lighting.md`, `visual-scorecard.md`; mechanics: `ui-ugui/SKILL.md`. Game-oriented suggestions for props, post-processing, movement and audio are intentionally excluded by the experimental specification. No character silhouette axis applies to this task. Visual review covers palette, consistency, spatial depth, UI hierarchy and preparation/practice feedback.
