# Warm study environment

User-selected direction, 2026-11-03: a welcoming study. This supersedes the earlier modern gray laboratory draft, which the user found reminiscent of an interrogation room. The gray draft was never a release.

- Style: a quiet reading room with warm plaster, natural oak, cream cabinetry, linen upholstery, books and restrained greenery. Open shelving and domestic furniture replace dark acoustic towers and exposed equipment carts.
- Palette: retain `LabPalette` for task/UI colors. Environment uses cream plaster `E9DFCB`, ceiling `E9E3D6`, oak tint `F5EAD5`, cream fronts `DDD2BA`, linen `C8BBA0`, muted foliage `65785A`, sage upholstery `89917A` and warm metal `8C806A`. Books have muted, unlabelled covers.
- Lighting: retain the fixed existing realtime key and ambient settings for dynamic task objects. Four fixed warm ceiling area lights and three baked practical lamps bake illumination/contact shading into static room lightmaps. The stimulus and movable task furniture are excluded from baking. No animated lighting, exposure changes, post effects, music or decorative motion.

The central stimulus, tray, task timing, response feedback policy and movie display geometry remain controlled. A narrow backing restores running-HUD contrast without moving text or changing content. New furnishings are static. This revision applies to every experimental condition and is recorded as `environmentRevision=warm-study-v1` in session metadata. Environmental warmth is a design intent, not a measured participant outcome. Physical headset comfort, distraction and performance require device acceptance.
